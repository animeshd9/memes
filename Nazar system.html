The Nazar system

Sigma’s logo and assistant are the same object. A protective eye that watches, listens, holds and celebrates, using nothing but its pupil, its sparkle and its rings. No face, no mascot, no costume.

01

The logo

Four concentric parts and one sparkle. Every state in this system changes one of these parts, so the mark always stays recognisable.

  • Ring · Electric Blue. The boundary that protects. Tightens when holding.
  • Sclera · white. Gives the eye its calm, open look.
  • Iris · soft blue. Moves to look, rotates to think.
  • Pupil · ink. Does all the expressing: dilates, slides, pulses.
  • Sparkle · the glint of life. Pops on waking, multiplies to celebrate.

Usage

Charcoal is home

The default background for the mark in-app and on the icon.

Beige for calm surfaces

Receipts, statements and onboarding.

Down to 16px

Below 24px, drop the sparkle outline so it doesn’t blur.

Never on orange

Orange is reserved for actions. The nazar on orange reads as a button.

Never turns red

Red belongs to the threat. The nazar is always on your side.

02

States

Each state changes one or two parts of the eye. Where I’m confident there’s one pose; where the right answer needs testing, there are two options, A and B, to try in the FGD.

03

Micro-interactions

Each one plays live on the stage, with the keyframes laid out underneath like a filmstrip, plus timing, easing and haptics for the build team. Hover or touch the “Moving with you” stage: the nazar follows you.

04

Outside the app

The nazar is how people know a message is really from Sigma. It appears in the same pose outside the app as inside, so recognition is instant and impersonation is harder.

A living app icon

Like Zomato and Blinkit for festivals, or Duolingo’s owl reacting to your streak. Four families: by season, by occasion, by what needs your attention, and by level.

Build note: Android can switch icons quietly using activity-alias entries. iOS shows a system alert every time an app changes its icon, so on iPhone the attention and level icons work best as an opt-in “Living icon” setting, with the same states also shown in the home-screen widget, which can update freely.
